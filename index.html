Cardiac Care 4 min read

What Is EECP And Who Can It Help?

EECP uses cuffs on the legs, timed with your heartbeat, to grow natural bypass vessels around blocked arteries. It is non-invasive, needs no hospital stay, and is used for angina and heart failure.

Cardiac Care 3 min read

Arterial Clearance Therapy (ACT) Explained

ACT uses EDTA to help clear calcified plaque and heavy metals from the body, supporting the heart, blood vessels, kidneys and blood flow to the brain.

Diabetes 5 min read

How Continuous Glucose Monitoring Helps

A small sensor on the skin tracks your sugar day and night, showing trends that a single finger-prick test misses and helping us fine-tune your diet and activity plan.
