Thyroid Management Program

The human body consists of a butterfly shaped thyroid gland at the base of your neck for regulating metabolic activities & functions your body. This gland may either under perform or over perform thus resulting in Hypothyroidism or Hyerthyroidism

Symptoms
Increased heart beats, feeling frightened, gidiness, weakness, depression, weight loss / gain, hair loss, irregular menstrual cycle etc

Thyroid Management Program
We usually take thyroid medication for longer periods of time, but the thyroid still remains uncontrolled as "We fail to change ourselves". Thyroid management requires a change in lifestyle, and this change should be personalized

Benefits
• Freedom from thyroid related complications & symptoms
• Decrease in medicine dependancy, or even a medicine free lifestyle
• Positive state of mind

Key Features
• Non medicine related concepts
• Diet
• Excercise
• Daily activity schedule
• Positive vision
• Customized and experience oriented
