Diabetes Reversal Program

Diabetes is the world’s fastest growing chronic condition. Insulin is needed to convert the glucose from our food into energy, which is essential for life. With diabetes, insulin is no longer produced, produced in insufficient amounts or is not sensitive enough to metabolize glucose. This leads to high blood glucose levels.
This could result in long term complications such as stroke, blindness, heart attack, kidney disease, and neuropathy. If not managed, this can impact quality of life and shorten life expectancy.

Let us work with you in taking care of your condition, thus ensuring an enjoyable life. We will look into:
• Education + counselling
• Healthy meal plan (with fruits and green vegetables)
• Physical activity + relaxation techniques
• Managing blood pressure
• Managing cholesterol levels
• Not smoking
• Cut down on alcohol
• We will help you develop confidence in looking after your diabetes
